- **D-381 (2026-10-13: an inner archive is stored as its own member object; the user's choice (a) of
  `nested-design.md` §2).** A declared nested archive (D-382) is a selected member of its parent, stored with a `member`
  origin and expanded from the store like any level-2 container. Each level is then a stored, content-addressed object,
  so `recompute`, `rederive`, `verify --level chain`, withholding and purge need no new case. The cost is the inner
  archives' bytes in the raw store (control-arena: 3.86 GB). A `raw_policy = include` release ships them (a later
  decision may mark them re-derivable and not deposited).
  - **Rejected:**
    - (b) a seekable inflate view over the parent member with `zlib` checkpoints. It was prototyped on the real 2.09 GB
      member (61 MiB, 1 s to index), but it needs a nested descriptor, an origin kind and changes to `verify` and
      `withhold`, and a `tar.zst` parent cannot use it;
    - (c) a depth allowance only (5 samples).
