- **D-34 (2026-10-11, v0.1 index facets, generic cell, logical key, evidence classes and oracles; phase 11).** The v0.1
  facets are the H.3.3 core facets only (`task`, `framework`, `model.requested`, `model.served`, the eight `n.*` counts,
  `generic_types`, `outcome`); adapter facets (`x.<ns>.<name>`) and `--pipeline` are deferred (the row validator already
  accepts namespaced members). `corpus/facets.py` computes them as pure functions of the stored AER and SEM documents, so
  nothing comes from an adapter. Each facet and cell member carries the weakest effective evidence class of the fields it
  is computed from; `outcome` elements carry their own `ev`. The cell is generic (H.7.4): the recorded `task` members,
  `suite_set`, `model`, `attack_params_sha256`, `repeat`. A member the AER declares unknown is `{"$unk": reason}`, and
  `logical_key` is `null` iff a member is unknown (else `"lk_" + b32(sha256("aer-lk/1\n" + JCS(cell)))[:26]`, recomputed
  by the row validator). `oracles` has one entry per regression-oracle spec with output. Rows hold no floats
  (non-integral numbers are `{"$dec": ...}`, H.1.4(3)). A row is a working row (the importer's) or a full row with all
  five members and every stage; mixtures are rejected.
  - **Consequence (disclosed).** `logical_key` is `null` where the benchmark actor's `version` is unrecorded (all of
    `agentdojo-upstream-2024-06`, about two thirds of head, a few AgentDyn rows) and for every `adprov-v1-release` row;
    an adapter-declared cell that could supply an inferred suite set is deferred. An execution whose header leaves
    `instrumentation` unrecorded cannot show an attack parameter in its cell. No AER, SEM or PROV byte changes.
  - **Why:** H.3.3(3) requires facets re-derivable from AER and SEM and invariant 1 forbids adapters from defining
    them; the weakest-input rule never over-states an epistemic status.
  - **Rejected:** adapter-supplied facets and cells now (needs a declaration mechanism in the shared registry);
    strongest or majority class for `ev`; treating an unrecorded `instrumentation` as unknown attack parameters (imports
    that do not record it could then never have a key); floats as strings or rounded; judge or auxiliary models in
    `model.*`; failing a row whose key cannot be computed (H.7.4(3) says `null`).
