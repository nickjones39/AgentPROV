- **D-892 (2026-10-28, measured and rejected: the SEC-22 determinism checks stay in the `serial` xdist group; D-401
  unchanged; M5 Ultra performance round 2, item 3; the user's decision, 2026-10-28).**
  - **The idea.**
    - `tests/security/test_sec22_derive_determinism.py` is 214 s of the `serial` group's ~386 s.
    - Per-worker timelines of `-n 24 --dist loadgroup` suites show that group running on one worker for the whole
      suite and finishing last. The other workers end within about 20 s of each other.
    - `docs/reports/suite-perf/baseline.md` lists the file under "own pools", not "load-sensitive".
    - So it was moved into the bounded `pools` groups, with `POOL_FILES` re-measured on the Mac Studio.
  - **Measured.** Three rounds, the commit before against the same tree with the move, `-n 24 --dist loadgroup`,
    loads 18–34:
    - wall time before 504.5 / 470.9 / 461.2 s, after 502.2 / 469.5 / 466.5 s;
    - with the move, `serial` ended at 281–307 s instead of last (368–406 s);
    - but the other workers then finished later: median worker end 369–402 s, against 339–375 s before.
    - Under real load the suite is bound by its total work and by kernel contention, not by its longest group. All
      three runs with the move passed.
  - **Decision.** Rejected: no wall-time gain, and it would have amended the user-approved D-401. `tests/conftest.py`
    is unchanged.
  - **Also rejected (item 3):** `--dist worksteal`. It was 11 % faster (393.5 s against 440.7 s mean, 3 runs each), but
    it ignores the xdist groups: the `serial` tests ran on 10 workers, up to 5 at once.
