- **D-10 (2026-10-08, local models and the `m5ultra` preset; user requirement, architect ruling).** Local
  OpenAI-compatible servers are first-class live endpoints: `ollama` is kept, and `omlx` (oMLX on Apple Silicon,
  `127.0.0.1:8000/v1`) is added. `--preset m5ultra` (shorthand `--m5ultra`) targets the user's Mac Studio M5 Ultra
  (80-core GPU, 256 GB). Unless `--jobs` is given, its concurrency is **computed from the model**:
  `min(J_compute(active params), J_memory(weights, KV per trace), J_server)` (MN N.2.1a). The table values are
  provisional, from published oMLX and SiliconBench data. They are calibrated on the machine with `--tune-jobs` in
  phase 8.

  **Rejected:**
  - a fixed number such as 4 or 18: concurrency depends on the model's size and context;
  - a hard-coded per-OS preset logic: presets are package data, consistent with D-7.
