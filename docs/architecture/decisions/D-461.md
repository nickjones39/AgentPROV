- **D-461 (2026-10-14, bug-pass fixes BF-19 and BF-15, routed from `bugpass-perf-10`; user decision 2026-10-14: approved, relayed by the orchestrator session).**
  - **BF-19 (security).** `verify --level chain` re-checks the AD-12 container closure of withheld members. It walks
    `raw/members.jsonl` upward from every object `WITHHELD.json` lists, through every level, and a released container
    of one is `withheld.container`. Before this, a container put back into a sealed tree while dropped from
    `WITHHELD.json` passed `chain --release`, and because it was deflated the rule rescan missed the member's bytes in it
    (`repro/BF-19_B-2`). The seal already withheld such containers (`corpus/withhold.py`, D-232); only the re-check
    was missing. Test: `tests/security/test_sec25_withhold_container.py`.
  - **BF-15.** `max_split_parts` drops from 64 to 32. The expand job holds every part open (D-243) and a worker may hold
    64 descriptors (`RLIMIT_NOFILE`, G.4.3), so split archives of 59 to 64 parts always failed, reported as
    `worker.input.missing` (`repro/BF-15_A-2`). 32 parts of 256 MiB are `max_split_bytes`, and the largest observed
    split has 8 parts. A worker that runs out of descriptors now reports `worker.input.nofile`. Tests:
    `tests/unit/raw/test_split_parts_nofile.py` (the invariant `max_split_parts` + 16 ≤ `nofile`, a split at the limit
    through a real worker, and the error code).
  - **Rejected:**
    - raising `RLIMIT_NOFILE` (it would weaken the G.4.3 worker containment, a setting with its own basis);
    - opening parts lazily or re-opening them without re-hashing (a second check-then-use window, and more code in the
      worker for no observed source).
  - **Downstream:** `ingest/limits.py` is an `EXPAND_MODULES` file and `IngestLimits` changes, so the `expand`
    `fn_digest` moves for every archive or container corpus at its next import, as does `limits_sha256` (a notice only
    since the D-31 addendum). No AER, SEM or PROV byte changes. `corpus/verify.py` and `io/workers.py` are in no stage
    digest. Regen-batch entry `bugpass BF-19/BF-15`.
