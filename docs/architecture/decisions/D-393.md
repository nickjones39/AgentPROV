- **D-393 (2026-10-14, core: progress channel 0.2; approved as direction 2026-10-14 by the user, relayed by the orchestrator session; build after the regeneration round).** `agentprov-progress/0.2`, additive to 0.1.
  - **New events:** `run` header, per-file `seq`, `stage` begin and end, a **`heartbeat` absolute snapshot** every 2 s,
    `worker` spawn and exit with pids, `session` (live, with the staging path), `control` acknowledgement, and
    `end.outcome`.
  - **File handling:** `--progress-file` **appends** and rotates at `--progress-max-bytes` (8 MiB, one `.1`).
  - **Gaps closed:** the T1 pool reporter is wired up, and `package` and `verify` gain stage events. Container imports
    mark `unit_kind`, so totals do not mix.
  - **Overhead** (research M1): ~1 µs per event enqueue, ~13.5 µs per line in the writer thread, plus one heartbeat
    every 2 s. That is under 0.01 % of an import.
  - **Byte-neutral:** no AER, SEM, PROV or `fn_digest` change.
  - **Rejected:**
    - parsing logs (prose; DEBUG cost);
    - polling `executions.jsonl` or `register.jsonl` (rewritten only at flush for `import`);
    - a socket or pipe protocol (a server; breaks re-attach).
