- **D-42 (2026-10-11, licence classes in deposits; the offline deposit builder and its archive format; phase 11;
  includes the release-scan rulings of the user).**
  - **Classes.** The class of a sealed corpus is `(licences.derived.spdx, sorted licences.restrictions)`, checked equal to
    the recorded `licences.class`. SPDX ids compare exactly (`GPL-3.0-only`, `GPL-3.0-or-later`, `AGPL-3.0-only` are three
    classes, D-25), and two corpora whose restrictions differ in any clause are two classes (`ast-survey-agentharm`).
  - **Refusals (exit 4).** Corpora of different classes in one deposit (`release.refused.mixed_licence_classes`) unless
    `--allow-mixed-licence-classes`, recorded in `deposit-metadata.json`, `DEPOSIT-MANIFEST.json` and a deposit note in
    each datasheet copy (the sealed corpus is never modified); a non-commercial class outside a corpus whose id ends `-nc`
    with policy `nc_partition` (an `-nc` class is never mixed, even with the override); relicensing a GPL/AGPL/LGPL or
    non-commercial source; raw policy `withhold`, licence policy `forbidden` or redistribution `not-permitted`; an
    unsealed, working or `.partial` tree; non-strict validation; an `interpreter_advisory` corpus; no release scan for the
    same root digest or an untriaged or `real` finding unless `WITHHELD.json` names its fingerprint (no scan result is a
    refusal, never a skip). Release mode below CPython 3.14.8 exits 3. A corpus with a `views/` tree exits 5 (views are
    not deposited in v0.1, AD-10 row 12).
  - **Offline by construction.** `agentprov.release` imports no network or process module and calls nothing named upload
    or publish (static, dynamic and behaviour tests). The scan, the verifier and the claims lint are injected
    (`scan=`, `verify=`, `lint=`). gitleaks 8.30.1 runs in `tools/release_scan.py` (`dir --redact
    --ignore-gitleaks-allow`, explicit default-rules config, reduced environment), so nothing inside scanned data can
    change the rules or hide a finding; no matched value is recorded; triage comes from a reviewed TOML file per
    fingerprint and per sealed root digest (`agentprov-release-scan/0.1`).
  - **Archive format** (the contract for archive-mode `verify`, D-38). `<id>-v<ver>.meta.zip` and
    `<id>-v<ver>.<stage>.<nnn>.zip` (stage `raw`, `aer`, `sem-<profile_dir>`, `prov-<spec_dir>`); regular files only,
    sorted bytewise, fixed `date_time` (release date, clamped to 1980-01-01), `create_system = 3`, `external_attr =
    0o100644 << 16`, DEFLATE level 6, no extra field, comment or data descriptor, no ZIP64 (below 2^31 − 1 bytes and
    65,535 members). Splitting is on shard boundaries on a deterministic upper bound of each member's stored size, so
    membership depends only on the corpus, never on the zlib version. Each member is re-hashed while written.
    `DEPOSIT-MANIFEST.json` (`agentprov-deposit-manifest/0.1`) and `deposit-metadata.json` (`agentprov-deposit/0.1`,
    `status: draft-unpublished`, `version_doi` always null, `concept_doi` and related identifiers only from `release.toml`
    and the sealed corpus) are written; nothing is uploaded or published. Two builds from the same inputs are
    byte-identical (Q.20), including from two independent copies of a seal.
  - **Release-scan rulings (user, 2026-10-11).** AgentPROV's own content-addressed keys (`derivationKey`, `subject_key`,
    `logical_key`) are known false positives: one committed allowlist (`_KEY_ALLOWLIST`) matches only the anchored
    `key":"(ex_|lk_)?<26 base32>"` text, so a secret elsewhere in the same JSONL record is still reported. AgentHarm's
    credential-shaped strings are fake benchmark values: the findings inside the AgentHarm content of
    `ast-survey-agentharm` (114 in the rehearsal) are triaged `synthetic-benchmark`, still per fingerprint and per root
    digest; home-path and host-name findings are not covered.
  - **Rehearsal** (`docs/reports/phase11/deposit-rehearsal.md`, on copies): Mandate sealed in 6.5 s (396 executions, 1,616
    files) and AgentHarm in 22.5 s (1,056, 4,262); each deposit built twice byte-identical (9.1 MB and 54.0 MB of
    archives); a Mandate plus AgentHarm deposit exited 4 and wrote nothing.
  - **Why:** the licence class is what decides whether corpora can share a deposit and a concept DOI; a draft that cannot
    publish and cannot reach the network keeps the release a human act (D-5).
  - **Rejected:** letting the override mix an `-nc` class (H.4.2 requires a separate deposit); splitting on actual
    compressed sizes (membership would depend on zlib); ZIP64 for the `2**31` default (H.10.4 forbids extra fields);
    running gitleaks from the builder (H.10.1 requires it offline with no subprocess); skipping a missing scan or
    verification silently; per-corpus triage of the generated keys (thousands per corpus); a line-level allowlist (a JSONL
    record is one line); copying `release-scan.json` into the deposit.
  - **Deferred:** `verify --level chain` before archiving and archive-mode `verify` of the deposit (D-38; U4 supplies
    them through `verify=`); a `synthetic` marker; separate concept DOIs per class (H.10.6).
  - **Allowlist widened for fragment cuts (2026-10-12).** gitleaks scans long lines in fragments, so a match can be
    cut at a fragment boundary (U4 rehearsal: `ivationKey":"…"`, a key without its closing quote). The allowlist
    accepts a field name cut at the start down to six characters and a key cut at the end down to 16 characters, and
    nothing else (never another full field name such as `activationKey` or `project_key`); 0 findings remain on the
    Mandate corpus.
