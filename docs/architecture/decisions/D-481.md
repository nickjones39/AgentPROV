- **D-481 (2026-10-14, core review D finding CD-4; user decision 2026-10-14: approved). A part served through another
  source does not count its registration in the coverage phrase.**
  - **Problem.** `coverage_phrase()` counted every registration with a counted `source` part, so AutoDojo counted through
    `rope-slice` (`via = "rope"`, corpus `rope`), whose logs are imported, owned and counted under ROPE. The phrase said
    7 sources; NC-AUTO-01 forbids presenting AutoDojo as a distinct corpus, and the `c3-coverage` gate counts over the
    pinned corpora, which give 6 (`repro/D1-4`).
  - **Rule.** I.5.6 rule 2: N excludes parts with `via` set; K is unchanged (the via part's dialect is counted through the
    serving source). Test: `tests/adapters/registry/test_claims.py::test_a_part_served_through_another_source_does_not_count_its_registration`.
  - **Rejected:** keeping N = 7 and reconciling NC-AUTO-01 and `c3-coverage` with it (it counts executions twice).
  - **Downstream: moves a manuscript number.** `all/coverage_phrase` and `all/n_sources_with_executions` drop from 7 to 6
    sources (3 formats unchanged). No AER, SEM or PROV bytes; `adapters/registry/claims.py` is in no stage digest.
