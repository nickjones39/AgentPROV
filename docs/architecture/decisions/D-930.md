- **D-930 (2026-10-28, `aprov` catalogue v3: an assessment cites no premise field declared unknown; user-approved
  2026-10-28 in the wave-2 session, "Yes go ahead"; byte class 2, as D-621).** The wave-2 imports of
  `credleak-p15b` · `head` and `dca-agentdojo` · `drift` each sealed one execution as `validate.failed` (tier 3,
  `pv.profiles.unk_premise`, NE-12). In both, a SEM `role` label rests on a `tool_execution`'s `/name` that the mapper
  declared `unk: ambiguous`, and `aprov.a_label@1` cited that field as `apv:sourcePointer`.
  - **The conflict.** SEM-V06 admits a premise declared in the record's `unk` (a label may rest on what the record
    does not know). NE-12 forbids a PROV statement whose field is in `unk`, and E.1.5 item 1 reads such a field as not
    present. The engine's node and edge rules apply E.1.5 item 1 (`Ctx.present`); the assessment rules' `premises()`
    resolved the field with `Ctx.val` and cited it unconditionally. The defect is the engine's, not the labellers'.
  - **Decision.** `aprov.a_label@2`, `aprov.a_payload@2` and `aprov.a_author@2` cite only the premise fields that are
    not declared unknown (`Ctx.unk_reason`, the pointer or an enclosing one). Each field left out is counted as
    `premise_field_unknown` in the assessment counters of the derivation report (E.7.3). The label itself is kept,
    with its SEM class: the claim is SEM's, and its evidence class already follows the SEM `ev` and rules (weakest
    premise, E.1.5 item 2). Premise annotations and the target are unchanged.
  - **Catalogue version 3.** The `aprov` catalogue goes from version 2 to 3. The three @1 rules stay for the documents
    that cite them. Version 2 (`99b6511a2d5e…`, which the eight sealed round-1 corpora and every wave-2 corpus built so
    far record) is archived byte-exact in `prov/catalogue/archive/agentprov-prov-0.1.v2.toml`; `corpus.authority`
    answers v1 and v2 from their archives. `validate.profiles` admits the @2 rules where @1 is admitted; the semdiff
    map targets both versions.
  - **Bytes (class 2).** The catalogue digest enters every bundle's derivation key (E.3.4), so every bundle IRI of a
    derivation at this commit differs from v2's, and every assessment statement of these three rules cites `@2`. No
    stored corpus changes: corpora keep the version they record and are reproduced at their building commit (F-8,
    D-430); a writing `recompute` or `derive` of a v2 corpus here refuses with version drift, so an upgrade is a fresh
    re-import. Pins regenerated: golden PROV, gate D, the DTap SEM/PROV digests and `docs/compat/semantic-diff.md`.
    `prov/compat/adprov_v2_3/` is untouched and its gate passes byte-identical.
  - **Scope of the rebuild (user, 2026-10-28).** Only `credleak-p15b` and `dca-agentdojo` are rebuilt, as new trees
    at this commit. The read-only NE-12 scan of DTap (the other wave-2 corpus whose mapper declares names `unk`) found
    0 of 60,553 executions exposed: its 20,300 `unk` names are claudesdk tool-result names that no label cites.
  - **Manuscript.** Datasheet item 23: under catalogue v3 an assessment never cites a field the record declares
    unknown, and the count of such omitted premises is reported per corpus.
