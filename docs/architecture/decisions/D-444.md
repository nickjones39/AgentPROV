- **D-444 (2026-10-14, GUI PROV graph appearance and interaction; user decision 2026-10-14: approved as direction).**
  - **Colour:** fills keep the W3C hues in Light and Dark. Strokes use the design §12 dark variants, plus 2-px
    Increase-Contrast strokes.
  - **Selection:** an accent ring plus a doubled stroke.
  - **Hover:** a `Qt.Popup` popover with a plain-text table; a click pins it to the inspector.
  - **Zoom and pan:** pinch zooms, smart zoom toggles Fit / 100 %, two-finger scroll pans, ⌥-wheel zooms, ⌘+ ⌘− ⌘0 ⌘9;
    the zoom range is 5–800 %.
  - **Controls:** glass controls over the canvas on macOS; plain buttons elsewhere.
  - **Rejected:** wheel zoom on trackpads (not macOS behaviour); recolouring the fills for Dark (breaks the notation's
    identity colours); selection by colour only.
