- **D-843 (2026-10-28, GUI Viewer: execution tab lifecycle, cap and memory; user decision 2026-10-28: approved as
  proposed in `docs/reports/gui/viewer-tabs/design.md`).**
  - A tab is keyed by (resolved corpus root, execution id); opening an open key focuses its tab without reloading.
  - At most 12 execution tabs (fixed, no Settings key, so no CLI-parity row). A 13th closes the least recently viewed
    tab, never the current one, and the status bar names it; "n of 12 open" is shown on the tab bar.
  - A tab reuses the `DetailCache` entry or loads in a worker; closing it drops late results. Only the current tab and
    the 3 most recently viewed keep a live graph scene; older tabs keep the layout result and rebuild the scene without
    re-running layout.
  - **Measured (2026-10-28, build log entry 53):** the parsed documents, not the scenes, are the cost. Twelve tabs of the
    largest agentdyn documents (~3.9 MB of PROV-JSON each) reach 687 MiB of RSS, over design §10's 300 MiB; twelve
    median ones (285 KB) reach 228 MiB. The user accepted this worst case on 2026-10-28; the cap stays at 12.
  - **Rejected:** no cap; refusing the 13th tab with a warning; a cap in Settings.
  - **Byte class 0:** `gui/` only.
