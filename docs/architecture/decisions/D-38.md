- **D-38 (2026-10-11, deposit verification: inputs and archive mode without extraction; phase 11).** `agentprov verify
  --corpus PATH` picks its mode from PATH without following a link: a directory holding `DEPOSIT-MANIFEST.json` is a
  deposit, a `<id>-v<ver>.meta.zip` is a lone meta archive, anything else a corpus tree (N.1.5). Archives are opened
  only through `agentprov.ingest.archives` (the G.3.5 gate) and members are streamed and hashed in memory; nothing is
  extracted. Member names are labels: they carry the corpus prefix `<id>-v<ver>/` and the H.1.2 grammar, and a label in
  two archives trusts neither. The union of a corpus's archives is the closed world of its own `checksums.sha256` (the
  archive-mode `ChecksumSource`); the deposit manifest (sizes, sha256, md5, `n_members`, `uncompressed_bytes`,
  `members_sha256`, root and content digests) is checked against it, never trusted for it. Levels: `chain` (default)
  runs unchanged over an archive-backed reader, `checksums` is available, `rederive` exits 5 (it needs a raw store on
  disk). A lone meta archive is checked against its own checksums and reports the rest `unverified`, never passed
  (`--release` fails it). Archive reading runs in the CLI process with the ingest limits (member bound raised to the
  deposit format's no-ZIP64 bound); a T1 `verify` job kind is a phase-12 follow-up. The deposit builder injects
  `verify --level chain`; `seal()` runs it too before the swap (N.2.11 step 3); `validate --corpus` stays read-only on
  working corpora (a departure from N.2.5(4): `derive` is the writer of the validation stage). Details:
  `docs/reports/phase11/decisions/D-38.md`.
  - **Why:** H.10.3(1) and G.3.5 forbid extraction; the corpus's own checksums and the published root digest are the
    authority, the deposit manifest is a claim to check.
  - **Rejected:** extracting to a temporary tree; a new T1 job kind now (`io/workers.py`/`worker.py` are phase 12's);
    trusting `DEPOSIT-MANIFEST.json` for the closed world; failing a lone meta archive outright; `rederive` by streaming
    raw members into a scratch store (extraction by another name).
