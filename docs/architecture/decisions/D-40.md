- **D-40 (2026-10-11, the corpus `ValidationSummary`: mode, per-check version and tier, tier results,
  `linkage_resolved_pct`; phase 11).** `corpus.json.validation` is computed at sealing from `validation/summary.json`,
  `validation/results.jsonl` and the `validation` stage of `derivation.json`: `mode` (`strict` when the stage ran in
  release mode; `package` refuses anything else), `checks` (`id`, `version`, `tier`, counts), `tiers` (per tier: checks,
  summed results, `executions`, `executions_passed`), `linkage` (`applicable`, `resolved` over the `pv.linkage.*`
  checks), `linkage_resolved_pct` and `libraries`. `linkage_resolved_pct = floor(10000 * resolved / applicable) / 100`
  written as the decimal `{"$dec": "100.00"}`, `null` when `applicable == 0`; the phase-10 corpora give `100.00`.
  - **Why:** the paper's claim `c1-linkage` and its validation table are read from the sealed corpus, not recomputed by
    the paper pipeline; floats are forbidden in the corpus layer (H.1.4(3)).
  - **Rejected:** a float percentage; rounding to nearest (99.996 % would read as 100.00); counting check results
    instead of executions.
