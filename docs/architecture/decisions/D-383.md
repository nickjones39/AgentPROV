- **D-383 (2026-10-13: streamed nested members, commit on verify).** A selected nested archive above the global
  `max_member_bytes` is streamed. The header carries `"streamed": true`, `DATA` frames follow as `zipfile` inflates it,
  and the closing JSON frame is `{"end": {"index"}}` (the CRC-32 and byte count checked) or
  `{"abort": {"index", "code", "reason"}}`. The parent publishes it only after an `end` and its own size and CRC-32
  check in `put_chunks`. After an `abort` the spool is discarded and the member is rejected with the code. Any other
  sequence is `expand.protocol`. The job's member cap is the largest raise of its selection. Spools go to the store's
  `tmp_dir` (`WorkerPool(spool_dir=)`) as unnamed files, so a crash leaves none; a stale named temporary of a dead
  process is swept when the store opens. Members at or below the global bound keep D-58's read-whole path.
