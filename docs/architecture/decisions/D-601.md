    - **`redact.py`.** `HARNESS_POINTERS` gains `/code/repo` (actor) and `/ext/inspect/error_retries` (scope). A
      `harness_text` rule may declare `pointers`: a non-empty list of distinct record-relative pointers, each a harness

      pointer that can hold either span: its lexical hints are float positions, and the keys under `revision` and
      `error_retries` are fixed, so nothing beyond BF-21's model-id handling is needed.
    - **Profile `whistlebench@2`.** `origin_userinfo` (pattern `(?<=://)[^/@\s"]{1,256}(?=@)`, `pointers` the two
      revision copies) replaces the userinfo with the literal `redacted`: no digest of a credential is recorded. The
      collision check of D.9.12 stays as it is, so a literal label admits one span per unit. A unit with two distinct
      userinfos fails closed (ProfileError, the unit is not mapped) and is never collapsed silently. The census has at
      most one per log. `retry_account` (pattern `(?<=/Users/)[^/\s"'<>\\]{1,64}(?=/)`, `pointers =
      ["/ext/inspect/error_retries"]`) gives the account segment `<account:{span12}>` (D-182). The label holds `<` and
