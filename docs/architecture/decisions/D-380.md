- **D-380 (2026-10-13, ingest-formats-3: container members of archive inputs; R-10p-6; user-approved 2026-10-13 with
  the deferrals session's review changes).** D-200's exit-5 refusal of an archive input of a container dialect is
  lifted. The archive input is expanded (D-200/D-249). Each selected container member (an Inspect `.eval`) is a
  candidate under its **chain label**: its member labels from the input down, joined with `!/`. It goes to
  `map_containers`, never `map_whole_files`. There it is expanded at its **absolute level** (`ContainerSpec.depth`: the
  level of its parent plus one, so a `.eval` directly in a split zip opens at 2), under its chain's depth allowance
  (`max_depth`, D-382), and mapped as from a tree. Profile globs (the reader's include, exclude, context and blob globs)
  are matched against each member's own label, the label `expand` matched at its level. The chain label is a register
  key only. Such a profile must declare `[import] include`. An `.eval`'s own expansion depends only on the reader's member
  patterns, so its member rows, objects and AERs are the same from a tree, a split zip, a nested zip or a `tar.zst`
  (differential test). Review changes, all built:
  - `derivation.json` stage maps are keyed by `(stage, fn_digest)` for `expand` (`corpus/stages.py`) in the importer
    (G4 check, document merge), `corpus/derive.py` (`_stages_of`, the H.8.4 stage comparison as sets of declared
    identities) and `corpus/verify.py`. One import records one `expand` entry per expansion function. G4 refuses a
    recorded selection under another expand function. A resume with another nested selection is refused (its profile
    changed). `package._stage` is never called with `expand` and is unchanged.
  - Candidates are routed per container dialect, asserted in the tests.
  - One subject rule: `ContainerSpec.prefixed` gives every member of an archive input the subject
    `<input>!/<chain>!/<member>`. No archive-input corpus is stored, so this moves no register byte.
  - A re-map (`recompute`, `verify --level rederive`) refuses an archive selection that the source's `derivation.json`
    does not record (`IdentityRefusedError`, exit 4). It compares selections, not profile digests: a re-map after a
    profile change that keeps the selection is the regeneration round's case (41d7a2f).
  - Carved blobs (D-218) are unchanged: a container unit's carve sources are the members of its `.eval`.
