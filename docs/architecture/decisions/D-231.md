- **D-231 (2026-10-12, withholding: the sealed tree is what is released; a private sidecar keeps the rest; user-approved 2026-10-13).**
  A withheld execution leaves the sealed corpus. Its index row leaves `executions.jsonl`, its validation row leaves
  `validation/` (`summary.json` is recomputed from the remaining rows by the writer's own function), and its AER, SEM,
  PROV and view files leave the tree. A withheld object's bytes leave `raw/objects/`. Its `raw/origins.jsonl` and
  `raw/members.jsonl` rows stay, and so does `raw/tombstones.jsonl`. Sealing **renames** these files into `DIR.withheld/`
  beside the sealed `DIR` (mode 0700): the same relative paths, plus the withheld index rows, their validation rows and
  a copy of `WITHHELD.json`. It never deletes them, rolls the renames back if sealing fails, and refuses an existing
  sidecar. The sidecar is not a corpus, has no checksums, and is never deposited; deleting it is the user's decision.
  The register keeps every entry of a withheld execution. Its `release.withheld` entry (stage `package`, disposition
  `withheld`) is added when missing.
  - **Consequence for the counts (H.6.1 R1/R5):** the frozen corpus-0.1 manifest has no `withheld` count, so a sealed
    corpus counts its released executions. `candidates == executions + not_imported` holds over them. The withheld
    executions are counted in `WITHHELD.json` and in `counts.issues_by_code["release.withheld"]`, and the datasheet
    states the number. `records_by_kind`, `models` and `framework_identities` cover the released AERs only.
  - **Why:** a deposit is the sealed tree, and archive-mode `verify` checks it closed-world. If a sealed corpus kept
    withheld files that the deposit lacked, the `checksums` level could not verify a deposit. Keeping the rows with
    their files gone would break every consumer that reads AERs (`stats`, `render`, `recompute`), and their facets could
    carry a fragment.
  - **Rejected:** a complete private sealed corpus with deposit-side filtering (a deposit that fails its own
    checksums); index rows kept in working form (consumers break, facets may leak); deleting the withheld files (the
    task rules forbid deleting data); a `withheld` count in the manifest (a corpus-0.2 schema for one integer).
