### CD-1: agentprov info truncates registry caveats at 500 chars, dropping the honesty statements of 7 parts

- **Area / severity:** D1, correctness. **Byte class:** 0. **Status:** fixed 79379c75.
- **Location:** src/agentprov/cli/info.py:133 (`return safe_text(value, 500)` inside `_escape`; also reached from line 144 `_registry` and line 308 `_sourc…
- **Invariant / spec:** none (D.3.5 rendering contract; HON-10 caveats)
- **Failing input:** The shipped registry. `_escape` passes every registry/profile string through `safe_text(value, 500)`, so `agentprov info` and `agentprov info --source X` (JSON and text) cut each caveat at 500 characters. Measured: agentdojo·head 642 chars, agentdyn·a 1001, autodojo·live 642, autodojo·rope-slice 555, rope·head 721, survey·agentdojo-runs 637, mandate·head 565 all end in `[truncated: N more chars]`
- **Expected:** D.3.5 / D.3.2 rule 1 (R-15, HON-10): the gated status is shown with its full caveat, generated from the registry. Escaping is fine, but nothing of the caveat should be lost
- **Actual:** The tail of each long caveat disappears. Lost text includes rope's 'no second import ... determinism across --jobs is not shown', agentdyn·a's 'SEM fails closed on the three new suites ... the stored derivation keeps @1 until the next re-derive', and autodojo·live's consent and ROPE non-reproducibility clauses. Readers see the status without the limitations that qualify it
- **Verification:** I reproduced this. `_escape` (src/agentprov/cli/info.py:130-137) passes every registry and profile string through `safe_text(value, 500)`. `escape_untrusted` then cuts the string at 500 characters and appends a '[truncated: N more chars]' marker. In the shipped registry, 7 strings are longer than 500 characters (the longest is 1001), and all 7 are part caveats. All 7 come out truncated, both from `collect()` (the text and `--format json` payload) and from `_source_info` (`info --source X`, e.g. agentdyn's caveat loses 501 chars).
