BF-1, BF-2, BF-6, BF-7 (workflow-tools); BF-3, BF-4 (suite-perf); BF-15, BF-19 (ingest-formats-3); BF-20, BF-32, the
BF-33 stop-gap (twins); BF-21, BF-22, BF-28 (regen-items-2-12). Proposals (`proposals.md`), all approved by the user 2026-10-14: regen-batch items 33..41 (BF-5, BF-23..BF-27,
BF-29..BF-31) and D-451 (BF-33), held for the regeneration round on branch `bugpass-regen`; D-450 (BF-18), built here.

Not findings (checked and left as they are): the D-412 strict-JSON depth fast path (a differential of the fast path
against the full check over 140,000 generated documents, 3 profiles and depth limits 1..8 and 256 found no difference); the D-407 precompile recompiling on every run (documented, about
1 s); the `live_home` session fixtures sharing one home on a worker (no failing case; xdist dispatch order keeps the
two backends apart).

Not covered by this pass (reviewers' own notes): the D-408 testmon lane, and not every one of the 86 session- and
module-scoped test fixtures was checked for mutation by its consumers (P1); fuzzing of zipfile internals beyond reading
the code (A); the views and compat code-digest scopes, the importer's G4 resume check, and `verify --level rederive`
sampling (D).
